04 The story

1989

Two trucks, one product, no grid.

  1. Two trucks in Dora

    Georges Karam starts delivering generator oil from a rented yard on the Dora highway, during the power cuts that never quite ended. Two trucks, one supplier, cash on delivery.

  2. The first INER-G blend

    Tired of imported oil that was never made for a 40 °C rooftop, the company blends GENSET 15W-40 with a partner in Cyprus. Within two years it is the brand.

  3. Our own plant in Chekka

    A blending and filling plant opens beside the port in Chekka: fourteen tanks, two lines and a laboratory run by one very strict chemist.

  4. Across the border

    First export pallets leave for Jordan and Iraq. Today INER-G ships to fourteen countries, most of them places with hot summers and long generator hours.

  5. The year the grid failed

    Demand for generator oil triples in one summer. The plant runs three shifts, adds four filling lines and never misses a rooftop delivery.

  6. The shop goes online

    Every product, every spec sheet and every filter reference, orderable from a phone. Same trucks, same drivers, fewer phone calls.
