02 Since 1989

Two trucks, one product, no grid.

Georges Karam started delivering generator oil from a rented yard on the Dora highway, during the power cuts that never quite ended. Thirty-seven years later the oil is our own blend, the plant is ours, and the trucks still leave Dora before sunrise.

1989
Two trucks and a rented yard in Dora, Beirut.
2004
Our blending and filling plant opens beside the port in Chekka.
2026
The shop goes online. Same trucks, same drivers, fewer phone calls.
