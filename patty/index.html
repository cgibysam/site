// 02 The craft

Three things we refuse to rush.

0grams per ball

The beef

80/20 chuck and brisket, ground every morning before the doors open. Never frozen, never pre-formed. Every ball is weighed by hand, because the scale doesn't lie.

0°C on the flat-top

The smash

Ten seconds under a heavy steel press. Maximum contact, maximum crust, and a lacy caramelised edge you can hear from the counter. One flip. Never early.

0seconds, ball to bun

The melt

One slice of American, because nothing else melts like it. Brioche from our own oven, toasted in beef fat. Ninety seconds from the griddle to your hands.
