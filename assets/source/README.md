# VELORNE Study 01 source

Original fictional watch model authored for this demo in Blender. Regenerate with `scripts/render/watch.py`. The `.blend` includes the assembly, materials, studio lights and camera. It is editable source, not manufacturing CAD.

Nine named assembly groups: crystal, hands, indices, dial, movement, case, crown, caseback and strap (bracelet).

Proposed visual design: graphite dial, small-seconds display, applied indices, faceted hands, fluted crown, dark center bracelet links, brushed shoulders and polished case edges. Model coordinates are arbitrary scene units. They establish no real dimensions or engineering specifications. The internal arrangement is illustrative and not a validated mechanical caliber.

No external product photographs, stock watch models, AI-generated product images or paid assets are used. Dial lettering is Blender text geometry. All imagery and the scroll sequence derive from this same scene.
